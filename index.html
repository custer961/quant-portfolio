01 · Market risk

Market Risk & Performance Report

Fetches daily prices for any symbol, then reports CAGR, annualised volatility, Sharpe, Sortino, max drawdown, Calmar, historical VaR, a correlation matrix and an equal-weight comparison — with charts rendered as hand-written SVG.

02 · Attribution

Trade Log Attribution

Parses a trading client's GB18030 logs, rebuilds the equity curve from the broker's own snapshots, and flags overtrading days, reversal trades, adding to losers and forced liquidations — each rule published.
